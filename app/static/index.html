Why the colours matter

01

Whisper already knows

The model reports a log-probability for every segment it produces. Almost every wrapper throws that number away and shows you only the text.

02

We keep it

That number becomes a 0–1 confidence per line. Below the threshold, the line is flagged. You see exactly where the model was guessing.

03

Silence gets caught too

Whisper will happily turn a pause into plausible text. A high no-speech probability flags those lines even when the words look confident.
